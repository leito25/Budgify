# Budgify demo script

## Setup

- Start the app with `npm start` and use the seeded workspace.
- Ask the participant to share their screen; do not ask for real banking credentials or account details.
- Observe without coaching. Record where they hesitate and any labels they misunderstand.

## Scenario

1. Enter a first name and note that this is a local sign-in, not account security.
2. Review the dashboard and identify the currency, debt estimate, and next payment.
3. Open Loans, add a small three-installment purchase, and find it with search.
4. Open the calendar, inspect the installment details, and mark one installment paid.
5. Switch to Income and explain recurring versus one-time entries.
6. Open Reports, download the amortization CSV, and open the print summary.
7. Switch to Spanish and confirm the interface changes while the entered loan name remains unchanged.

## Observation checklist

- Could the participant start without help?
- Did they understand that rates, payment amounts, and late interest are estimates?
- Could they find the loan, calendar, and report actions?
- Did they understand that the name-only sign-in does not protect their data?
- Record screen, step, participant's words, and a suggested improvement. Do not record financial or other sensitive personal details.
